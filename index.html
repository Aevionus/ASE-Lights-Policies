Privacy Policy for ASE Lights

App name: ASE Lights (ASE-Lights)

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Contact: info@aselights.com

ASE Lights is a mobile app for controlling compatible WLED lighting devices on your local network. An account is required. This policy describes the information the app collects, how it is used, who receives it, and how you can delete your account.

ASE Lights does not sell personal information and does not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

1. Information we collect

Account

When you register, we collect your email address and a password. The password is handled by our authentication provider and is not stored in the app’s profile database. You can also save a first name and last name.

Address and precise location

You can save a physical address (street, city, state or province, country, and postal code), a time zone, and precise coordinates (latitude and longitude).

If you allow location access, the app uses your location only while it is open, and only to:

  • fill in your address;
  • determine sunrise and sunset times for lighting schedules;
  • send coordinates to a light you choose, so that light can calculate sunrise and sunset.

You can type an address instead of using GPS. The app does not collect location in the background.

Lighting devices

To find and control your lights, the app may store the device name, local IP address, host name, MAC address or other device identifier, LED configuration, presets, schedules, and status such as brightness, power, effect, colour, and whether the device is reachable.

Wi-Fi credentials

During setup, you may enter your home Wi-Fi network name and password so the app can join the light’s setup network and send those credentials to that light. The Wi-Fi password is sent only to the light on your local network. It is not saved in your ASE Lights account.

Biometric sign-in

If you turn on Face ID or fingerprint sign-in, your device authenticates you locally. ASE Lights does not receive or store your biometric data. A sign-in session may be kept in the device’s secure storage.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive the address you write from and whatever you include in the message, such as your device type, app version, and a description of the problem.

2. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • create and secure your account;
  • discover WLED-compatible devices on your local network and control them;
  • save and apply presets, schedules, and device settings;
  • calculate sunrise and sunset times and set a light’s location and time zone;
  • sync your profile and device records across phones you sign in on;
  • respond when you contact support.

3. Who we share information with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We share it only as follows:

  • Supabase provides account authentication and the database that stores your profile, device links, and device-related presets. Data sent to Supabase uses HTTPS. Supabase’s privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google receives address text and coordinates when you search for an address, reverse-geocode a location, or look up a time zone (Places, Geocoding, and Time Zone). This is used to fill your address and set schedule times. It is not used for advertising. Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Your WLED light receives local network requests, including coordinates and, during setup, the Wi-Fi name and password you enter. Those requests use HTTP on your local network because the controller does not support HTTPS. They are not sent to ASE Lights’ servers.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, or if necessary to protect the security of the app, our users, or our services.

Apple and Google distribute the app through their stores. Their own store privacy practices are separate from this policy.

4. Local network access

ASE Lights needs local network access to discover and communicate with WLED devices. That communication stays on your Wi-Fi network unless you set up remote access yourself.

5. Analytics and advertising

The production app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not use the advertising identifier. If that changes, this policy will be updated before those tools are shipped.

6. Storage and security

Profile and account data are stored by Supabase. Some settings and the sign-in session are stored on your phone. Traffic to Supabase and Google uses HTTPS. Traffic to a WLED controller on your LAN uses HTTP.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Retention

We keep account and profile information for as long as your account exists. Information stored only on your phone remains until you delete the app, clear its data, or remove it in the app. Configuration stored on the WLED controller remains on that controller until you change or reset it.

8. Delete your account

You can delete your ASE Lights account in the app or by email. Deletion removes the account. It is not a temporary deactivation.

In the app: open Account Settings and tap Delete Account. This deletes:

  • your sign-in account (email and authentication record);
  • your profile, including name, address, time zone, and saved coordinates;
  • the cloud record of which lights were linked to your account.

Presets and schedules stored for a lighting controller are kept with that controller so the light can keep working. They are not a separate copy of your profile. Settings that exist only on the controller stay there until you change them on the device or factory-reset it. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you delete the app or clear its storage.

On the web: email info@aselights.com with the subject “Delete my ASE Lights account” and the email address of the account. We will delete the same account, profile, and device-link records from our active systems. We do not keep the account for marketing. If a backup copy exists, it is not used and is removed on the provider’s normal backup cycle.

9. Children’s privacy

ASE Lights is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live. Contact us if you believe a child has given us personal information and we will delete it.

10. Your choices

  • You can deny location permission and type an address instead.
  • You can turn biometric sign-in off in Account Settings.
  • You can remove a saved light in the app.
  • You can delete your account as described above.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
